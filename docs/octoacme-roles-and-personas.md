# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers shape user experiences by conducting research, creating designs, and validating usability. They bridge the gap between user needs and technical implementation, ensuring products are intuitive and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and pain points through interviews and usability testing
- Design and validate user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine requirements based on user feedback
- Work with Developers to ensure design feasibility and iterative implementation
- Conduct user testing and gather feedback at key milestones
- Maintain design system documentation and consistency

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centered solutions
- Reduce friction in user workflows and improve satisfaction
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design practices

### Interactions
- **With Product Managers**: Validate design concepts against business goals and user stories; refine requirements through collaborative workshops
- **With Developers**: Iterate on implementation details; provide guidance on UI components and interactions; review code against design specifications
- **With QA/Testing**: Provide acceptance criteria for usability; participate in UAT feedback
- **With Project Managers**: Update on design timelines and dependencies; coordinate design reviews and validation phases

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design documentation and style guides

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain the infrastructure, tooling, and processes that enable reliable, scalable deployments. They collaborate closely with Developers to automate testing, deployment, and monitoring.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage development, staging, and production environments
- Implement infrastructure-as-code for reproducibility and version control
- Monitor system performance, reliability, and security
- Automate testing, deployment, and rollback procedures
- Collaborate with security teams to embed security scanning and controls

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and time-to-market
- Maximize system uptime, scalability, and observability
- Reduce manual toil and enable self-service for teams

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Review code for deployment readiness; automate testing in PRs; troubleshoot deployment issues; provide runbooks and monitoring dashboards
- **With QA/Testing**: Ensure smoke tests and acceptance tests run in CI; coordinate staging environment readiness
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate deployment schedules, maintenance windows, and rollback procedures; escalate infrastructure risks early
- **With Releases/On-call**: Participate in release planning; support incident response and rollbacks; document post-mortems

### Typical Communication
- CI/CD pipeline status and alerts
- Infrastructure documentation and runbooks
- Deployment coordination and release notes

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical delivery by refining requirements, validating acceptance, and ensuring alignment between stakeholders and the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into clear, actionable user stories and acceptance criteria
- Support backlog prioritization by assessing business impact and dependencies
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business objectives
- Clarify business logic and edge cases during development
- Participate in UAT and acceptance testing

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver measurable business value
- Reduce rework by catching misaligned requirements early
- Improve communication between business and technical teams

### Interactions
- **With Stakeholders**: Gather requirements; validate scope and prioritization; communicate delivery status and impacts
- **With Product Managers**: Refine user stories; validate acceptance criteria; support backlog grooming
- **With Developers**: Clarify complex business logic; answer questions on edge cases; support technical design discussions
- **With QA/Testing**: Participate in UAT; validate acceptance criteria; support test planning
- **With Project Managers**: Update on requirement changes and impacts; flag scope risks; support planning and estimation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- Acceptance criteria validation and UAT reports
- Business impact assessments and change logs
