# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

Welcome! This README provides an overview and quick navigation to the project management processes practiced at OctoAcme.

## Process Summary

OctoAcme's project management is structured around five phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Close & Retrospective**. The process prioritizes customer-first thinking, iterative delivery in small increments, and clear ownership with defined roles (Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, QA, Stakeholders). 

Projects begin by validating business needs and aligning stakeholders (Initiation), solidify work scope and timeline (Planning), and move into day-to-day execution with a strong emphasis on visibility and quality: daily standups, weekly syncs, and rigorous pull request/QA workflows with automated and manual testing (Execution & Tracking). Risks and dependencies are actively managed, escalated through clear triage pathways, and regularly communicated via status reports and dashboards (Risks & Communication). All releases require thorough preparation, stakeholder communication, and post-release validation per a standardized checklist (Release & Deployment). Each project or sprint ends with a retrospective to identify improvements and feed them back into process refinements (Retrospective & Continuous Improvement).

Quality assurance is baked in—from PR policies (automated tests, reviews, linking issues) to end-to-end smoke tests, with roles and responsibilities spelled out for every action. Metrics such as velocity, burndown, and project health signals are tracked throughout. All process documentation is open and versioned in this repo to support transparency, repeatability, and faster onboarding.
